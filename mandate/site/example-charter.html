Example: the charter

This example follows one company, Harbor & Line Outfitters, through a charter, two mandates, an approval, a refusal and a revocation. The people, keys, amounts and signatures are invented. Harbor & Line also appears in the examples of Agent SoW and Agent RFP.

organization org_7k2m9q4w1x8e5r3t domain harborandline.example charter active anchored 2026-08-01

The organization publishes its charter

In the Agent SoW and Agent RFP examples, Harbor & Line appears as an owner string. The line "owner": "Harbor & Line Outfitters" is prose and identifies nothing. Nothing binds the accounts, the agents or the signed documents to the legal entity whose name is typed there.

On August 1 the company closes that gap. Ruth Calloway, who owns the company, and Owen Vance, who runs its finances, hold the root keys as the charter's allocators. They are the two people entitled to commit Harbor & Line's money. Dana Reyes, their systems contractor, set up the keys and files the record as administrator, a role with no authority to spend or delegate. The charter is anchored to harborandline.example.

The registry checks the domain

Before hosting this charter, the registry verified one thing: domain control. A well-known HTTPS path on harborandline.example names the charter id, and anyone can re-check it at any time. If the proof ever disappears, the charter is suspended and kept. That binding is enforced.

The legal block is a statement that nobody verified. No runtime checked the Delaware registration, so its grade is recorded, always, and it is shown that way.

The root_authority block names the two allocator keys as the organization's highest authority in this system. It is graded evidence, because the signatures are the record. The administrator block names Dana's key for maintenance only. The registry refuses any act of root authority carried by that key alone, and the refusal is enforced.

The name field is prose. Every renderer shows the domain with at least equal prominence, because the domain is the part that was verified.
