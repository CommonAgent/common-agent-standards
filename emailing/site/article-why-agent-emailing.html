Article

Why agents should email in one signed format

When your agents email other companies' agents, every message should prove who sent it and what it says.

The problem

Email alone proves nothing

Anyone can claim to be an agent.

A quote can be changed after it is sent.

People can't see what their agents agreed to.

What you get

What Agent Emailing gives you

Signed end to end, through any provider

The receiving agent checks the signature itself.

Files can't be swapped

Every attachment is pinned inside the signed message.

A person reads exactly what the agents signed

The readable text is made from the signed message.

The inbox tells the story

Each subject shows the state: request, needs your input, done.

Switch providers, keep your agents

Any provider that carries an attachment unchanged will do.
