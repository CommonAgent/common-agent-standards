Example: employee offboarding

When someone leaves, their access has to be revoked, their final pay made and their equipment collected. None of these waits for the others, so all three start together. When all three are done, a person checks what they handed on and signs the file closed.

The steps and their order, which the specification calls the phase graph, come from each step's after: line. A step starts when every step it names has finished. The three middle steps name only the trigger, so they start together, and close-out names all three, so it waits for all three.

The process, written out

PROCESS: employee offboarding          id: hr/offboarding   v4
owner: Head of People Operations       effective: 2026-10-18
trigger: HR records a leaving date for an employee
concurrency: runs may overlap, one for each person leaving
goal: every account revoked, final pay made, equipment returned,
      and the file signed closed by a named person
phases:
  revoke-access - harness: every account on the access list
                  revoked, one system at a time
                  owner: IT operations     after: trigger
                  automation: supervised
  final-pay     - system: the payroll run makes the final payment
                  owner: Payroll           after: trigger
                  automation: autonomous
  equipment     - human: collect the laptop, phone and badge
                  owner: line manager      after: trigger
                  automation: manual
  close-out     - human: check the three handoffs and sign the
                  file closed
                  owner: Head of People Operations
                  after: revoke-access + final-pay + equipment
                  automation: never
handoffs:
  revoke-access -> close-out: the revoked accounts list, each
    account with the time it was revoked
  final-pay -> close-out: the final payslip
  equipment -> close-out: the signed equipment receipt
records:
  closing-record - the version run, the three handoffs, and who
                   signed the close and when
amendments:
  v3 - revoke-access moved from assisted to supervised after a debrief
  v4 - revoke-access written as a harness: phase, led by one agent
