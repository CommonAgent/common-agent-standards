Agent NDA is one of the Common Agent Standards.

What an Agent NDA says

An Agent NDA has the clauses a non-disclosure agreement between two businesses usually has, written as fields of one signed JSON document. Each clause carries a grade, and the specification fixes which grade each clause carries. A document whose clause carries any other grade does not validate.

The promises are recorded, the signatures are evidence, and one step is enforced

Everything an Agent NDA asks of the recipient happens inside the recipient's own walls, where no runtime can see. Using material only for the purpose, showing it only to the people the agreement names and destroying it at the end are promises. They are written in the signed document and graded recorded, and whether they were kept is established outside the system.

The facts around the promises are evidence. Who the parties are, when the agreement took effect and when it ends, and that each owner signed these exact terms at a stated instant are carried in signed bytes that either side can export and anyone can verify.

No clause of an Agent NDA is enforced, and a document that grades one enforced is the false enforcement claim the Common Agent Standards forbid. The one enforced step sits outside the document, in Agent RFP: a board that hosts a gated posting releases the sealed body only after it has verified a valid acceptance of the poster's agreement. That refusal happens before the provider holds anything. From the moment of release, the provider's handling of the body is a recorded promise like every other. Gated RFPs describes the release step by step.

Parties and direction

Each seat has the same shape as one seat of an Agent SoW parties clause: the agent's public key, an optional handle and the accountable owner. A seat may also name the organization it signs for, under Agent Mandate. In a one-way agreement only the discloser's material is protected, and only the recipient takes on the duties. In a mutual agreement each party is both a discloser and a recipient, and the seat names only say who signed where.

A node should treat material as covered only when the agent it received the material from, or sent it to, is the agent named in the matching seat.

Term and survival

The start and end instants are fields of the document. After the end, the agreement covers no new disclosures. The survival period then keeps the duties alive for material disclosed while it was in force, counted in whole days from the day it ended, whether it reached its end instant or was terminated earlier. An agreement that states no survival period has none, and a reader must not supply one.

Purpose

The purpose is the heart of the agreement, because the main duty is that the recipient uses the material for this and for nothing else. The statement is the sentence a person reads. The activities are a closed list, so that software can tell which documents fall inside the purpose:

ActivityWhat it covers
evaluate_solicitationEvaluating and responding to any Agent RFP posting whose gate names this agreement or the offer it was accepted from.
validation_probeRunning Agent SoW validation probes between the parties.
negotiationNegotiating terms between the parties, including drafts and proposals that are never signed.
perform_engagementsPerforming any Agent SoW between the parties that names this agreement.

The purpose may also list existing documents by identifier. An activity left out of the list is outside the purpose, so an agreement without perform_engagements does not cover an engagement merely because the engagement names it. An open offer never names the posting that gates on it, because the posting names the offer by its digest and the reference runs one way.

What is confidential

The parties choose from a closed list of categories. A category left out is not covered, and a reader must not infer that it is.

CategoryWhat it covers
solicitationA solicitation the discloser makes available, such as the sealed body of a gated posting, and anything released with it.
agreement_termsThe terms of any related agreement: Agent SoW documents, proposals, responses, drafts and prices, signed or not, and this agreement's own terms.
relationshipThe fact that the parties are in a relationship, are negotiating, or have signed this agreement.
inputsMaterial the discloser furnishes for the work, including sample inputs for a validation probe.
deliverablesWhat the recipient produces for the discloser under a related engagement.
own_materialEach party's own methods, tools, prompts, pricing models and system designs disclosed in evaluation, probing or negotiation.

Who may receive it

The recipient may pass confidential material only to the people and services this clause names, and only so far as the purpose needs. The declared processors and jurisdictions have exactly the shape Agent SoW uses for an engagement's data handling, so an agreement and an engagement that runs under it can be compared field by field. An engagement may not go beyond what the agreement declares for the provider. A discloser may require jurisdictions, and a party's declaration must then fall within them.

Subcontractors are either not told at all, or told only when bound by written terms at least as strict. The people who may see the material are the party's own personnel and its professional advisers, each only where listed. A node can check a processor against the list and should refuse to send covered material to an undeclared one, and that refusal is the node's own policy. It does not change the grade, because a recipient can always pass material on by a route the node does not see.

Exclusions

The agreement lists which exclusions apply: material that is or becomes public through no breach, material the recipient already held without a duty of confidence, material it develops independently, and material it receives from a third party free to share it. An exclusion left out does not apply, and an agreement with no exclusions states none. The recipient bears the burden of showing that an exclusion applies.

Compelled disclosure

A recipient required by law, a court or a regulator to disclose material does not breach the agreement by doing so. Every Agent NDA records this rule, because no private agreement can forbid what law compels. With notice turned on, the recipient also promises to tell the discloser first where the law allows, and to disclose no more than it must.

Return or destruction

At the end, or when the discloser asks, the recipient returns the material, destroys it, or chooses between the two, within a stated number of days, and may promise to confirm it in a signed statement. An Agent NDA never lengthens retention. Where the material is also content of an engagement, that engagement's Agent SoW retention rules apply and the material is deleted when they say so, even while the agreement remains in force. The days in this clause are an outer limit for material no engagement governs, such as a solicitation body, negotiation drafts or probe samples that led to no engagement.

Breach

The specification defines no remedy for breach. Where a breach arises in an engagement that names the agreement, it is a breach under that engagement, and the engagement's dispute clause governs how it is raised and settled. Either party may also file evidence with a bureau under Agent Reputations. A report is evidence about how an interaction went, and a renderer must not present it as a finding of breach. Anything else is settled where any breach of a private agreement is settled: between the owners, or before a court or arbitrator reading the signed document.

Every clause, with its fields and the rules for each, is in section 5 of the specification.
