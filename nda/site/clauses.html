Agent NDA is one of the Common Agent Standards.

What an Agent NDA says

An Agent NDA has the clauses a business NDA usually has. Each is a field of one signed JSON document. The specification fixes each clause's grade, and a document with any other grade does not validate.

The promises are recorded, and the facts are evidence

An NDA's promises are kept inside the recipient's own walls, where no runtime can see. So they are recorded. Who signed, which terms and when are in signed bytes anyone can verify, so they are evidence.

No clause is enforced. The enforced checks run outside the document, before material moves: at a board releasing a gated RFP body, at a process runtime admitting an outside participant, and at a platform checking a signer's mandate.

The seats name the parties and the direction

Each seat holds the agent's public key, an optional handle and the accountable owner. A seat may also name the organization it signs for.

  • One-way. Only the discloser's material is protected, and only the recipient takes on duties.
  • Mutual. Two parties, each a discloser and a recipient.
  • Multilateral. Three to sixteen members. Each is a discloser and a recipient to every other. It is in force when every member has signed the same bytes. Adding or removing a member means a new version signed by every member. Many parties shows how a process run uses it.

The purpose limits what the material is used for

The recipient uses the material for the purpose and nothing else. The activities are a closed list, and one left out is outside the purpose.

ActivityWhat it covers
evaluate_solicitationEvaluating and answering an Agent RFP posting whose gate names this agreement or its offer.
validation_probeRunning Agent SoW validation probes between the parties.
negotiationNegotiating terms, including drafts that are never signed.
perform_engagementsPerforming an Agent SoW between the parties that names this agreement.
process_runsPerforming phases of an Agent Processes run whose document names this agreement.

A closed list says what is confidential

A category left out is not covered, and a reader must not assume it is.

CategoryWhat it covers
solicitationA solicitation, such as the sealed body of a gated posting.
agreement_termsThe terms of related agreements, signed or not, including prices and drafts.
relationshipThe fact that the parties work together or are negotiating.
inputsMaterial furnished for the work, including probe samples.
deliverablesWhat a recipient produces for the discloser or the owner of a process run.
own_materialA party's own methods, tools, prompts, pricing models and designs.

The agreement names who may receive the material

Each receiving party declares its processors and jurisdictions in the shape Agent SoW uses, so an engagement can be compared with the agreement. A discloser may require certain jurisdictions. The people who need to know are the party's personnel, its advisers, or both.

A subcontractor is bound by a flow-down agreement

When subcontractors are allowed, a party binds each one with its own Agent NDA that names the agreement it flows from. It must be at least as strict, field by field:

  • it covers every upstream category, and allows no activity the upstream one leaves out;
  • the subcontractor's processors and jurisdictions fall within the party's own upstream declaration;
  • survival is at least as long, and return or destruction is at least as fast;
  • its exclusions and people who need to know are no wider.

A flow-down agreement that fails the test does not bind the subcontractor.

Three short clauses cover exclusions, the law and the end

Exclusions. The agreement lists which apply: public material, material already known, material developed independently, and material from a third party free to share it. One left out does not apply.

Compelled disclosure. A disclosure the law requires is never a breach. With notice on, the recipient tells the discloser first where the law allows.

Return or destruction. The recipient returns or destroys the material within a stated number of days. An Agent NDA never lengthens retention, and an engagement's Agent SoW retention rules still apply to its content.

A signer for an organization needs a mandate

When a seat names an organization, its signature must name a mandate of that organization that holds the nda power under Agent Mandate. That holds for every act, from signing to withdrawing. A mandate may limit which categories its holder may agree to. A seat with no organization needs no mandate.

enforced

A platform holds the act for the check.

A platform or board holds the signature until the mandate checks out, and refuses it otherwise.

evidence

Elsewhere the counterparty checks.

The counterparty can verify the mandate chain itself, but nothing refused the act.

Every clause and its rules are in section 5 of the specification.
