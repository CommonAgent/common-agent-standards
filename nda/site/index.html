Agent NDA

An Agent NDA is a signed non-disclosure agreement between two parties whose agents work together, written so that software can read it and grade each clause.

Organizations often make a provider sign a non-disclosure agreement before it may even see a request for proposal, because the RFP holds strategic information: what the organization plans to change, what it spends today and which suppliers it is unhappy with. Confidential material also changes hands in an Agent SoW validation probe, where a client sends real sample inputs before anyone has signed a statement of work, and during negotiation, while drafts and prices pass back and forth. And one non-disclosure agreement often covers a whole relationship, across many statements of work signed over years.

A clause inside an Agent SoW cannot cover any of that. Before a statement of work exists there is no document for the clause to sit in, and once several exist, each would carry its own copy of the same promises with its own start and end. So Agent NDA is its own signed document, and the documents that depend on it name it by its identifier and digest.

One agreement covers three moments

A non-disclosure agreement is usually signed early and outlives the first piece of work. An Agent NDA is built for the three points where confidential material changes hands between agents.

  1. 1

    Before an RFP is shown

    A buyer publishes a gated posting under Agent RFP: a public summary anyone can read, and a sealed body held back. The posting names the buyer's Agent NDA, signed as an open offer. A provider accepts by signing, and the board releases the body only after it has verified that acceptance.

  2. 2

    During a probe and a negotiation

    Before anyone signs a statement of work, a provider can run an Agent SoW validation probe on the buyer's real inputs to show it can do the work, and the two sides trade drafts and prices. The same agreement covers all of it.

  3. 3

    Across many contracts

    When work begins, each Agent SoW names the agreement it runs under by identifier and digest. The agreement keeps its own term, so it outlives any one engagement and covers the next.

Every clause says what backs it up

The Common Agent Standards label every clause with one of three grades: enforced, evidence or recorded. A non-disclosure agreement asks the recipient to do things in private: to use material only for a purpose, to show it only to certain people, to destroy it at the end. No runtime can watch any of that. So an Agent NDA uses only two of the grades, and a document that labels any of its clauses enforced does not validate.

evidence

Who signed, and when

The parties, their keys and owners, the instants the agreement starts and ends, and the signatures themselves. Anyone can verify them from the signed bytes, and a dispute starts from that record.

recorded

What the recipient promised

The purpose, what is confidential, who may receive it, the exclusions, compelled disclosure, how long the duties survive, and return or destruction. They are signed and citable, and a breach is settled between the owners, under a related engagement or before a court.

enforced

One step, outside the document

A board releases the sealed body of a gated posting only to a provider whose acceptance it has verified. That refusal happens before the provider holds anything, so it is enforced at the board. Agent RFP defines it, and Gated RFPs walks through it.

Who it is for

  • Buyers and their counsel who already require a non-disclosure agreement before they share an RFP or sample data, and want their agents to ask for one and check it.
  • Providers whose agents read RFPs, run validation probes and negotiate, and who want to accept an agreement without a person retyping it into another system.
  • Implementers of boards and runtimes that host gated postings or hold confidential inputs, and need a document they can parse, verify and cite by digest.

An Agent NDA describes what the parties recorded and signed. It is not legal advice, and what a court would make of a particular agreement is a question for the law that governs it.

Where it fits among the Common Agent Standards

Agent NDA is the confidentiality document of the contracting standards. Each of the others has a part in it.

StandardIts part beside an Agent NDA
Agent RFPDefines the gated posting: the public summary, the sealed body, the open offer the posting names by digest, and the board's release of the body after it verifies a provider's acceptance.
Agent SoWDefines the validation probe and the statement of work. An Agent SoW names the agreement it runs under by identifier and digest, and its own data-handling clause still governs retention for that engagement.
Agent MandateShows who may sign for an organization. A party can name the organization it acts for, and a signature can name the mandate under which the signer acted.
Agent ReputationsHolds evidence of how parties behaved. Either party may file evidence of a breach with a bureau, and a party considering an agreement may read the other's record first.
The Common Agent SpecificationDefines the agent itself, with the key that signs and the identity both sides verify.
